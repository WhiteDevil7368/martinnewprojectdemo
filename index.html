Four commitments

We want a job market where the rules are clear, enforced, and the same for every employer.

Workforce protection

Employers should recruit in good faith and give qualified Americans a real chance at every opening.

Visa integrity

H-1B and F-1 OPT programs work best when the rules are followed and violations are investigated.

Fair hiring

Every applicant deserves a transparent process free of unlawful bias.

Corporate accountability

Companies that break labor and immigration law should face the penalties the law provides.

About us

Mission. Advocate for American workers, protect domestic employment opportunities, prevent visa fraud, promote fair hiring, and hold corporations accountable for unlawful employment practices.

Vision. An economy where lawful hiring is the norm and American workers and graduates can build careers in their own country.

We support lawful immigration. We oppose visa fraud and the unlawful displacement of workers. Our target is rule-breaking, never any group of people.

Fairness

One standard for every employer.

Integrity

Claims backed by cited sources.

Accountability

Enforcement through lawful channels.

Opportunity

Good jobs for American workers.

Our priorities

Hire American workers first

Promote good-faith recruiting, internal training, and local hiring pipelines.

Stop H-1B visa fraud

Support enforcement against sham positions, benching, wage violations, and falsified filings.

Strengthen F-1 OPT oversight

Back stronger employer reporting and verification so training programs serve their stated purpose.

Protect American STEM graduates

Help new graduates reach entry-level roles and fair evaluation.

Prevent unlawful worker displacement

Document and challenge layoffs that violate the law or program rules.

Corporate accountability

Push for transparency, penalties, and public records of violations found by agencies.

Fair hiring and equal opportunity

Equal opportunity for all lawful workers, with no discrimination by citizenship or national origin where the law prohibits it.

Enforce U.S. labor laws

Wage, hour, safety, and anti-retaliation protections apply to every worker.

Report suspected visa fraud

Important: A report here is not an official government complaint. We do not investigate or prosecute. To trigger an investigation, file with the agencies listed below. A suspicion is not a finding, so please describe only what you directly saw or know.

USCIS – Report fraud U.S. Department of Labor – Wage and Hour complaints DOJ Immigrant and Employee Rights Section EEOC – File a charge ICE HSI tip form

Privacy notice. We ask only for what is needed to review a report. Evidence may contain personal data, so remove information about third parties that is not essential. See our Privacy Policy.

Share your story

Were you laid off, passed over, or displaced? Tell us what happened. A reviewer reads every submission, and nothing is published without your explicit consent and administrator approval.

Stories describe personal experience. They are labeled as such and are not presented as proof of any employer’s wrongdoing.
